Privacy Policy

Last updated October 9, 2026

Overview

BYTEXIO LLC built Sparky AI. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. We keep it short because the app is designed to collect as little as possible.

Information we collect

Sparky AI doesn't collect personal information. We don't run analytics, advertising or tracking, and we don't sell data. What you enter in the app stays on your device, and is only sent to services you set up yourself (such as your own server).

Data on your device

Settings and content are stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your private iCloud account, which Apple protects. Deleting the app removes the data on that device.

Permissions

If the app asks for access to something (for example the local network, photos or notifications), it's only used for the feature that asked, and you can change it any time in Settings.

Children

Sparky AI isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date below.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@bytexio.com.
